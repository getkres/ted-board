Demo Terrain

Archived USGS terrain is a demonstration, not putting guidance. Only Haggin Oaks, Alister MacKenzie hole 1 has terrain here. Other greens show outlines and yardage only.

USGS 3DEP Upper South American / Eldorado 2019 data was collected in 2019 and 2020. Samples use a 1 m raster and 2 m Gaussian-smoothed derivatives. This is neither a current green survey nor a measured cup position. GPS and map alignment can be several meters wrong.

Arrows show broad downhill direction. Heat categories: cyan below 1%, yellow from 1% to below 3%, and orange at 3% or more. Neither mode predicts a putt or an aim point.

Course geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The bundled course dataset preserves its source metadata. Archived terrain provenance and raster hash are bundled in slope-data.js.

Location stays in memory on this device. There is no analytics, account, location upload, or location storage. An online first load must finish before the shell and data can be available offline. Hardware GPS, sunlight readability, input comfort, and battery life still need testing.
