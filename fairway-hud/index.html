What this view can tell you

The bottom-left map appears within 30 yd of the nearest green edge and hides beyond 35 yd. Front, center, and back yardages stay in the bottom-right corner. The map turns so the line from you to the pin points up. Pin position defaults to the mapped center. At less than 0.5 m separation, the map stays north up.

The lens panel shows only yardage and the conditional green map. The map contains the outline, a cyan player dot, and a white pin. Arrows show broad downhill demo terrain by default; heat is selectable. Archived USGS DEM is not putting guidance. Typical 3 to 5 m GPS error on a green can move your dot across a whole putting line. Reported GPS accuracy is an estimate, and tapping a pin does not remove map or GPS error.

Front and back are the green edges on a line from your location through its mapped center. Center is the polygon's area centroid. Distances are horizontal. The green mini-map fits both you and the full green at a uniform scale.

Choose the hole yourself. Live values and the map are hidden when a fix is older than 20 seconds, reported GPS error exceeds 30 m, or you are over 5 km from the selected green. On a phone, use HTTPS and allow location. Position stays in this browser. Archived terrain is a demo, not putting guidance. This Web App has no AR tracking.
